Questions

The honest answers.

Where do the photos come from?

From you. We use your real photos, pulled from your website and your Google Business profile, plus anything you send us. Your feed shows your products and your shop, not stock imagery of someone else's chocolate.

Do I get to approve things before they go out?

Always. Every month you get one link on your phone showing the full month of content. Approve it all in two minutes, or flag anything you want changed. Nothing publishes without your yes.

Who owns the content?

You do. Every post, caption, and reel we make for you is yours to keep and reuse, even if you cancel.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime, no contract, no wind-down fee. You keep everything already delivered.

Is this AI-generated junk?

No. It's your products, your photos, and copy written about what you make and sell. You don't have to take our word for it: ask for a free 3-post sample built from your own photos before you pay a cent.
