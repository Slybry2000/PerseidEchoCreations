Questions

The honest answers.

Where do the photos come from?

Yours first, pulled from your website and your Google Business profile, plus anything you send us. Where you have no photograph for something, we design imagery that matches your brand and we tell you which is which. You will never find a stock photo of someone else's bakery on your feed.

Is a robot writing this?

Software drafts it and a person reviews every month before it reaches you, and then you review it again before anything publishes. That is how one operator can do this well for $299 instead of $1,500. Judge the free three posts and decide for yourself.

Do I approve things before they go out?

Always. Once a month you get a link on your phone with the whole month on it. Approve it all in two minutes, or flag anything you want changed. Nothing publishes without your yes.

Who owns the content?

You do. Every post, caption and reel is yours to keep and reuse, even if you cancel.

What if it is not working?

Cancel from your Stripe receipt, or email us and we will do it. No contract, no wind-down fee, and you keep everything already delivered.

Do you do this for my kind of business?

Bakeries, coffee roasters, chocolate shops, creameries, distilleries and the shops around them. Food and drink you can photograph is what this is built for. If you are outside that, say so and we will tell you honestly whether we are a fit.

Who you are dealing with

Second Rise is run by Bryan Piard, out of Renton, Washington. It is a small operation on purpose. You will not be handed to an account manager, there is no onboarding sequence to sit through, and the person reading your reply is the person building your posts. That is also why there is a ceiling on how many shops we take at once, and why the free sample is the fastest way to find out whether this is for you.
