A website that explains a new app in one visit

The Green Felt Club is my own scoring app for pool players. The site had one job: let a stranger understand it and try it without a sales call.

The problem

A new app has to answer three questions fast: what is it, is it for me, and what do I do next. Pool players, league organizers and venue owners each ask those differently, and none of them will read a long page to find out.

What I did

  • Designed, wrote and built the site myself, so the words and the layout were made for each other.
  • Made a short demo film from the real product, showing a player joining with a QR code and the scoreboard on a venue TV.
  • Gave organizers and venues their own pages, so each visitor lands on the version written for them.

What changed

The site does the explaining. A visitor can watch the product work before talking to anyone, which is the same job I build sites to do for clients.

Work it takes off a person

Explaining the product from scratch on every call. Sending screenshots by email to show what it looks like.
