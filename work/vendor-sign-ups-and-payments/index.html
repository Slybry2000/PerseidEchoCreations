Vendor sign-ups and payments without the email chase

A home care technology buyer's guide needed vendors to sign up, pick a listing and pay, without someone managing each one by email.

ScopeInvitation emails, sign-up tracking and payment collection
Client's wordsFewer steps to send invitations
Client's wordsAutomated payment receiving

The problem

Getting a vendor into the guide meant a string of manual steps: find the right contact, send an invitation, track who answered, fix bounced addresses, then arrange payment. Each vendor was a small email thread, and there were a lot of vendors.

What I did

  • Built vendor invitation emails that go out from one place instead of one at a time.
  • Added sign-up tracking so it is clear who has joined, who has not, and who needs a nudge.
  • Set up payment collection inside the sign-up, so a vendor can join and pay in one sitting.
  • Kept the pages and listing categories easy to change, so the guide can grow without another build.

What changed

The publisher describes it as fewer steps to send invitations, and payments that arrive on their own. Those are the client's own descriptions, not measured results.

Work it takes off a person

Chasing updated contact emails, bouncebacks and replies by hand. Sending payment details one vendor at a time.
