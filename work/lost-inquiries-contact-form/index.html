The contact form that was losing every inquiry

A group travel company had a "Begin a conversation" form on its site. It looked fine. It captured nothing.

BeforeInquiries opened the visitor's own mail app and were never recorded
AfterEvery inquiry is saved and emailed to the team within 15 minutes
AlsoNewsletter sign-ups go straight onto the right list

The problem

The form on the site did not send anything. Pressing the button opened the visitor's email program with a blank message. Anyone on a phone without mail set up, or anyone who closed that window, was gone. Nothing was saved anywhere, so nobody knew how many people had tried.

The newsletter checkbox next to it did nothing at all.

What I did

  • Replaced the form with one that records every inquiry the moment it is sent.
  • Set up a check that runs every 15 minutes and emails each new inquiry to the team. Replying to that email goes straight to the person who asked.
  • Connected the newsletter checkbox to the company's existing mailing list, kept separate from its other audiences.
  • Added a "Book a call" button beside the form for people who would rather talk.
  • Later rebuilt the front page as three panels so each kind of trip gets equal space.

What changed

Inquiries now arrive in an inbox instead of disappearing, and each one can be answered by hitting reply. The owner no longer has to wonder whether the site is working.

Work it takes off a person

Checking whether the form works. Copying newsletter sign-ups onto a list by hand. Chasing people who thought they had already written in.

Disclosure: Dream Travel is a family business. No lead counts are quoted because the old form recorded none to compare against.
