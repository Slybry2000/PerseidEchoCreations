Example, made up

What the note looks like

This is a fictional shop and a fictional job, written to show the format. It is not a client.

What the owner sent

“We quoted a deck repair and the customer approved it by text. Nobody scheduled it. Two weeks later they called angry and left a review.”

Where it broke

The approval came in on a text thread only the estimator saw. Nothing moved it to the scheduler, and no one owned the job between “yes” and “on the calendar.”

Two fixes to try first

  • One named owner for every approved estimate until it is on the calendar
  • An automatic message to the customer within one business day saying when the work is booked

What I need

Two sentences, no customer names
