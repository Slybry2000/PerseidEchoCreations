What it is like

One form, one link a month, and almost no meetings.

Being a client is deliberately boring. Here is the whole of it.

  1. You fill out one form

    Ten minutes, once. What you do, who buys from you, and anything you would never want said in your name. That is the entire onboarding.

  2. We build the month

    Copy, graphics, carousels, video. You are not asked for assets, ideas, or a content calendar. If we need something only you have, we ask once.

  3. You get one link

    Everything for the month in one place. Tap approve on what you like. Tap send back with a note on what you do not. Most clients spend about ten minutes here.

  4. It posts itself

    On schedule, to every platform, without you logging into any of them. Nothing publishes that you have not approved.

  5. You get a recap

    One page at the end of the month: what went out, what people responded to, what we are trying next. Read it or do not.

How it starts

You do not pay until it sounds like you.

Voice takes a couple of rounds to land. So the first two weeks are calibration, and they are free.

  1. Five posts

    Built from a short intake form. No kickoff call, no questionnaire marathon. You mark what lands and what misses, one tap per post.

  2. Five more, tuned

    We adjust to what you marked. This is usually the round where it clicks.

  3. Five more, dialed

    By now it either sounds like you or we are not the right fit. Either way the fifteen posts are yours to keep.
