Questions owners ask

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Posts can be built from your photos, your listings, your work, or simple designed graphics.

What if I don't like a post?

Say so and it gets changed or dropped. Nothing posts without your yes.

Which platforms?

Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn to start. Others when they fit your customers.

Do I own it, or am I renting it from you?

You own and control what is built for your business. You get the credentials, the configuration, the documentation, and a plain-English owner's guide: everything needed to run it or hand it to someone else.

You are one person. What happens if you disappear?

Fair question. Everything is documented well enough for another competent person to pick it up, monitored so problems announce themselves instead of going quiet, and built on tools you can access without me.

What happens to my data?

Before anything is built, you get it in writing: what the work touches, which outside services process it, what is stored and for how long, and who has access. If any of that is not acceptable to you, we design around it or we do not build it.
