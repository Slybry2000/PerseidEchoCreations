What is a Workflow Opportunity Map?

The short answer

A Workflow Opportunity Map is a $750 working engagement where I take one process in your business apart and write it up. What you get

Eleven sections, one workflow

It's a working session with me and then a written document, not a slide deck. This is what the document covers.

  • How the work actually happens today, mapped step by step
  • What the customer experiences at each step, and what that creates for you
  • Where it breaks, stalls, or gets dropped
  • What can safely be automated
  • What should stay human, and why
  • The approval and safety boundaries the system would run inside
  • Tools, access, and dependencies it would need
  • The recommended first fix, and whether it needs building at all
  • How we would measure whether it worked
  • Implementation scope and a price range
  • A phased roadmap for anything that comes after

What it costs

$750, and it comes off the build

The Map is $750. If you sign a build within 30 days, the $750 is credited toward it. Builds start at $2,500 and most land between $3,500 and $9,000 depending on how many systems have to agree, and the Map is where the real number for yours comes from.

A lot of people in this line of work will spend two unpaid hours designing your system, and that's really a sales meeting. I would rather charge a small fixed fee and hand you something you can use, including the parts that argue against building anything.

Whether or not I build it

You keep the map

If you take it to someone else, or build it in-house, or decide the honest answer is to fix the process before automating anything, the document still does its job. It's yours either way.
